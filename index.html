The problem

A single job produces dozens of files. The answers are scattered across all of them.

Drying logs, adjuster summaries, moisture readings, equipment invoices, and job photos live in different folders, apps, and inboxes. When an insurer asks why an item is on your estimate, someone has to dig through every one of them to find the proof.

What Prism does

From a pile of files to a job you can explain.

01

Every file. Stored together.

Upload a job’s estimates, reports, receipts, and photos as they are. Prism keeps the originals in one place, organized by job, so nothing lives in a stray inbox or shared drive.

02

Your files, read and understood.

Prism processes PDFs, including scanned pages, and JPEG or PNG photos. It extracts text, recognizes each document’s role, and pulls out individual estimate items so you don’t have to.

03

Specific, traceable connections.

Select an estimate item to see the reports, receipts, and photos that support it, down to the page and quote. Direct references are kept separate from AI-inferred support, so you always know which is which.

How it works

Three steps. No new paperwork.

  1. Upload

    Drop in the files you already produce. No new forms or templates.

  2. Prism processes

    Every page and photo is read, classified, and linked to the rest of the job.

  3. Trace the evidence

    Open the job graph and follow any item back to its source when it matters.
